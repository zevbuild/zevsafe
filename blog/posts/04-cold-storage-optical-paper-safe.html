Cold-Storage Optical Paper-Safe & QR Recovery

1. Why Digital Backups Fail: The Cold-Storage Imperative

Storing master encryption keys or recovery phrases in cloud notes, password managers, or text files on your desktop creates a single point of failure:

  • Malware, info-stealers, or browser session hijackers can exfiltrate text files.
  • Cloud accounts can be locked, compromised, or subject to credential stuffing.
  • Digital drives suffer from silent bit decay or electronic controller failure over years of storage.

The Ultimate Defense is Cold-Storage Paper: An archival physical document stored in a fireproof home safe or safety deposit box cannot be hacked remotely, infected with ransomware, or probed by automated bots.

2. Generating the Printable HTML Certificate

When encrypting a folder in ZevSafe:

  1. Click Generate Paper-Safe Recovery Sheet in the master passphrase card.
  2. ZevSafe opens a high-contrast printable document modal with security Guilloche dashed borders.
  3. Click Save Offline Certificate (.html) or Print Certificate.
  4. The Cold-Storage Rule: ZevSafe never renders your actual password into the printable HTML. Instead, it provides dedicated handwriting boxes so you can write your Diceware words using an archival indelible pen.
🛡️ Zero-Knowledge Printing Principle

Modern office and home wireless printers contain internal hard drives and print spool caches that log printed images. By requiring you to write your Diceware password by hand, ZevSafe guarantees that your master password is never spooled to a printer hard drive!

3. Under the Hood: The Zero-Dependency QR Engine

Unlike standard web apps that call external Google Chart APIs or load heavy 150 KB third-party QR libraries from CDNs, ZevSafe includes an in-tree pure-JS engine (js/qr-engine.js):

  • ISO/IEC 18004 Standard: Implements Byte Mode data encoding in pure JavaScript.
  • Galois Field Arithmetic: Performs Galois Field GF(256) polynomial division for standard Reed-Solomon error correction.
  • Dynamic Version Scaling: Scales automatically from Version 1 up to Version 14 based on payload size with 8-mask penalty scoring.
  • Pure Vector SVG: Outputs clean, scalable SVG vector elements without canvas memory allocations.

4. Understanding the 8-Group Salt Fingerprint

Every recovery sheet features an 8-group formatted salt fingerprint:

Cold Storage Parameter · Formatted Salt Fingerprint
SALT FINGERPRINT:
7A9B-4F12-88C0-E315-99A1-D42B-6F01-C73E

KDF SPECIFICATION:
PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 · 600,000 Rounds · 256-Bit Master Key

This allows you to visually verify whether a physical recovery sheet belongs to a specific .zsafe (or .zev) file without attempting decryption.

5. Restoring Vaults: Camera Scanner & Clipboard Paste

When restoring a vault from a printed recovery sheet:

Method A: In-App Optical Camera Scanner

  • On the Decrypt screen, click the 📷 Scan QR button.
  • Grant camera access. The hardware-accelerated W3C BarcodeDetector (with pure-JS fallback) scans the QR code in under 100 ms.
  • Salt parameters, iteration counts, and vault bindings are instantly populated.

Method B: Optical Clipboard Paste (Ctrl+V)

  • If you took a photo or screenshot of the QR code (using Windows Snipping Tool, Win+Shift+S, or Mac Cmd+Shift+4), simply press Ctrl+V anywhere on the Decrypt screen.
  • ZevSafe inspects your clipboard image in local browser memory, extracts the QR code, decodes the parameters, and applies them immediately.
