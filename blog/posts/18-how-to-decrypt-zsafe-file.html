How to Decrypt and Explore a .zsafe File: Complete Walkthrough

1. Quick Decryption Steps

Unlocking a .zsafe file requires no desktop software installations, decompression tools, or command lines. It runs 100% locally in your web browser:

⚡ Core Mental Model

[ 1 📦 Select .zsafe File ] → [ 2 🔑 Enter Passphrase / Scan QR ] → [ 3 📂 Explore Files Instantly ]

2. Step 1: Loading Your .zsafe Vault File

Open zevsafe.pages.dev and switch to the Unlock a Vault card:

  • Drag and Drop: Drag your .zsafe (or legacy .zev) vault directly into the teal drop zone.
  • Browse File Dialog: Click 📦 Select .zsafe File to choose the vault from your file manager.
  • Double-Click (PWA): On desktop Chrome/Edge, if you installed the ZevSafe PWA, you can double-click any .zsafe file in your OS file manager to open it immediately in ZevSafe.

3. Step 2: Entering Passphrase or Keyfile

Once the vault is loaded, provide your credentials:

  1. Enter your master password in the passphrase field.
  2. If the vault was locked with a 2FA keyfile, ZevSafe detects the flag in the 57-byte header and highlights the keyfile selector. Attach your keyfile file.
  3. Click 🔓 Unlock Vault. ZevSafe derives the master key and decrypts the encrypted tail catalog in under 300 ms.

4. Step 3: Optical QR Code 1-Scan Auto-Unlock

Did you generate a physical Paper-Safe Recovery Sheet? You can skip manual typing:

  • Click the 📷 Scan Paper QR button.
  • Point your webcam at the printed QR code, or paste a photo of the QR code from your clipboard.
  • ZevSafe's dual-engine scanner (native BarcodeDetector + pure-JS jsQR) decodes the parameters and triggers 1-Scan Auto-Decryption, unlocking your files instantaneously.

5. Step 4: Exploring Files in the Web Vault Explorer

Upon unlocking, ZevSafe launches the full-screen Decrypted Vault Explorer directly in browser memory:

  • Directory Hierarchy: Browse folders, subfolders, and nested files with a breadcrumb bar and folder tree.
  • Search Filter: Filter thousands of files instantly by name or file extension.
  • Single-File Download: Click the ⬇️ Save button on any file row to export just that specific document without extracting the whole vault.
  • Full Archive Export: Click 📦 Download Decrypted ZIP to extract the complete unencrypted directory as a standard ZIP file.

6. Step 5: Streaming Video & Floating Audio Deck

You don't need to download or extract massive media files to play them:

Click ▶️ Play on any video (MP4, WebM, MKV) or audio track (MP3, WAV, FLAC). ZevSafe's streaming demuxer feeds decrypted byte ranges into a local HTML5 video element on demand. You can seek forward and backward across a 5 GB movie instantly with zero buffering lag and zero RAM inflation.

7. Step 6: In-Vault Live Document Editing & Re-Encryption

Need to update a private note, code snippet, or markdown document?

  1. Click on any text, markdown, or code file in the explorer to launch the In-Browser Editor with line numbers.
  2. Make your edits. ZevSafe stages your changes inside an ephemeral in-RAM overlay map.
  3. Click 🔒 Re-Encrypt (.zsafe) in the top explorer toolbar. ZevSafe packages your updated files into a fresh .zsafe container without touching disk!

8. Step 7: Locking Your Session & Memory Scrubbing

When you finish browsing your private files, click the 🔒 Lock Session button:

🛡️ Instant Forensic Memory Wiping

ZevSafe immediately scrubs all derived cryptographic keys from memory, revokes all internal blob: streaming URLs, and zeroizes plaintext byte buffers. Your browser tab returns to a clean, locked state with zero forensic traces left on your device.
